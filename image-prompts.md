## Notes for the Photographer

- All images should be shot on a full-frame camera with a prime or short telephoto lens (35mm–85mm equivalent)
- Colour grading: neutral-to-warm, clean — avoid heavy LUT treatments or overly desaturated looks
- Subjects should be real professionals in real work environments — avoid overly posed or stock-photography aesthetics
- Clothing: smart-casual to business casual; avoid bold patterns
- Avoid: lens flare effects, artificial bokeh overlays, neon or blue-tinted office lighting
- Preferred: natural window light, honest candid moments, real documents and screens visible
- All faces should be appropriately released for commercial use
