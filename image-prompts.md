# Image Prompts — greaterheight.tech

All images sit inside `assets/images/`. Drop the generated file at the path shown and it will appear automatically — no code changes needed.

Prompts are written for a professional editorial/corporate photographer. Avoid cinematic effects, AI movie aesthetics, or stock-photo clichés. Shoot natural light where possible; controlled studio or office light elsewhere. Subjects are real people in real environments.

## Notes for the photographer / image generator

- All subjects should reflect Nigerian professional demographics unless the prompt specifies otherwise.
- Clothing should be smart-casual or business formal — no casualwear.
- Avoid wide-open mouths, exaggerated expressions, or anything resembling a stock-photo pose.
- Avoid heavy vignettes, lens flares, cinematic colour grades, or AI-art aesthetics.
- Target colour temperature: 5500–6000 K (daylight/office) unless stated.
- All hero images should work at 16:9 with the central subject offset slightly left of centre, as a right-side gradient overlay will cover approximately the right third on desktop.
