Offshore Delivery Model

 

Offshore delivery model provides cost-effective benefits to clients in need of extended team that can work in offshore development center to add value to their business.

In the case of the offshore delivery model, clients can handover all their IT requirements to the offshore team that works remotely to complement client’s onshore/onsite team and develop the most suitable product or solution. Our development centers act as a dedicated unit, offering client’s specific requirements for the best possible solutions. In the offshore delivery model, get the benefit of reduced costs with high-quality work. With no additional expenses, you can complete your business achievement without worrying about the resources. Choose an offshore delivery model to avail of the benefits of cost savings and reduce risks associated with the execution.

 

 

 

Hybrid Delivery Model

 

Hybrid delivery model is the amalgamation of onsite and offsite models. It offers the best of two models with benefits of optimum cost and fast turnaround time.

The hybrid delivery model is best suited for clients wanting to take advantage of direct interaction with skilled professionals along with availing cost-effective benefits. Some portion of work is done at the onsite center and the rest is carried out at the offshore development center. However, the distribution of work completely depends on the type of project. Optimize costs and maximize efficiency by combining the best of onsite and offshore models. While the initial planning stages of product or solution are executed onsite, subsequent development and support phases are carried out offshore at the Hidden Brains development center.

Offsite / Offshore Delivery Model

 

Outsource the project to the nearest service provider and let clients control the development process to reap great benefits from offshore outsourcing.

In the offsite/offshore delivery model, client’s project is distributed between the offsite center and an offshore development center which can be located in different countries. Under this situation, the offsite center acts as a mediator between the client and its offshore development centers. This is one of the most successful and popular outsourcing delivery models that divide the tasks between two teams for faster execution and quick results. Fast response time, maximum control on your project, and affordable costs are major advantages of choosing an offsite/offshore delivery model.

 

 

 

Global Delivery Model

 

The global delivery model is the best suitable one for clients that need constant interaction, real-time control, and monitoring of the progress of the project.

The global delivery model can be considered as one of the most demanded and preferred outsourcing delivery models. Clients can expect real-time control of the project as per their comfortable time zone.

By choosing Greaterheight's global delivery model, the client can experience several benefits such as comprehensive reach, internationally accepted procedures and insights, innovative solutions, and highly experienced, skilled resources. Through our 24*7 services, we provide development services globally as per your specific time zone and location.

 

 

Onsite Delivery Model

 

The onsite delivery model is perfect where the scope of the project is repetitive, open-ended, and requires our skilled personnel to work on client’s premises in line with their in-house team.

With the onsite delivery model, clients can get full support and time of Hidden Brains skills professionals to build and implement solutions of varying complexities. It becomes possible for the client to engage in a face-to-face discussion with onsite professionals for the scope of the project, challenges faced, and ways to address them. Clients can suffice their organization’s IT requirements through a team working onsite to give a thorough understanding of their business needs. This model is the best choice for developing products or solutions that are iterative in nature and require all relevant stakeholders to be in close contact with each other.
