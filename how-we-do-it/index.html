Delivery Methodology

Process That Removes Risk From Every Engagement

We do not operate on assumptions and guesswork. Every project moves through a defined sequence of phases — with clear accountability, regular checkpoints, and quality gates that prevent defects from reaching production.

Discuss an Engagement See Delivery Models
  • Structured Delivery
    Defined phases, milestones, and quality gates from discovery to deployment.
  • Dedicated Teams
    Senior engineers and specialists assigned per project — not rotated across dozens of accounts.
  • Transparent Communication
    Weekly reports, live dashboards, and direct access to your team — never filtered through account managers.
The Delivery Lifecycle

Six Phases From Brief to Live

Every engagement follows the same structured path. What changes is scope, team composition, and timeline — the rigour at each gate does not.

01
Discovery
Structured requirements gathering, stakeholder interviews, technical assessment, and scope definition. Outputs a detailed SOW before any code is written.
02
Architecture
System design, technology selection, data modelling, infrastructure planning, and security framework — reviewed by a senior architect before sign-off.
03
Development
Two-week Agile sprints with daily standups, peer code review, CI/CD pipelines, and end-of-sprint demos to keep stakeholders in the loop throughout.
04
Quality Assurance
Automated unit and integration tests, manual exploratory testing, performance benchmarking, and security scanning before any build moves to staging.
05
Deployment
Staged rollout with canary releases or blue-green deployments, rollback capability, and infrastructure-as-code so environments are reproducible and auditable.
06
Support & Growth
Post-launch SLA-backed monitoring, incident response, and regular review cycles to improve performance, add capability, and respond to user feedback.
Our Framework

Agile — With the Discipline of a Structured Practice

We use Agile as a framework, not a philosophy. That means we take what works — short iterations, continuous delivery, adaptive planning — and pair it with the documentation, governance, and quality controls that enterprise clients expect.

Our approach is deliberately scaled: startups get the lean version; enterprises get the full governance layer. Both get daily accountability and a predictable outcome.

Sprint Planning & Backlog Management
Prioritised backlog, story-point estimation, and two-week sprints with a defined definition of done reviewed with the client before the sprint starts.
Daily Standups & Async Updates
15-minute synchronous standups for the team, supplemented by end-of-day async updates in the client's preferred communication channel.
Sprint Reviews & Retrospectives
Fortnightly demos with the client stakeholder, followed by internal retrospectives to identify and act on process improvements immediately.
Continuous Integration & Delivery
Automated build pipelines trigger on every commit. Only builds that pass all test gates and code review can progress to staging or production environments.
Change Management & Version Control
Git-based branching strategy, mandatory PR reviews, and a formal change-request process for scope additions that protects timeline and budget.
Quality at Every Gate

How We Protect Quality Throughout

Quality is not a final check — it is enforced at each phase boundary so that defects are caught where they cost the least to fix.

Automated Testing
Unit, integration, and regression test suites run on every commit. Coverage thresholds are enforced — builds below the minimum do not advance to review.
Security Scanning
Static application security testing (SAST) and dependency vulnerability scans run automatically in the CI pipeline, with findings triaged before any production release.
Performance Testing
Load and stress testing against agreed performance benchmarks before each major release, with results documented and shared with the client.
Peer Code Review
No code ships without a review from a second engineer. Complex sections require a senior review. This is a hard gate, not a guideline.
UAT & Acceptance
Structured user acceptance testing with the client team, managed in a shared test-case register. No deployment until all acceptance criteria are signed off.
Accessibility & Compliance
WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility review on all user-facing interfaces, plus applicable compliance checks (GDPR, NDPA, PCIDSS) depending on the product's scope.
Keeping You Informed

Communication That Builds Confidence

No Black Boxes. No Surprises.

We believe clients should always know exactly where their project stands. Not in a weekly digest they have to decode — in a format that gives them genuine visibility into progress, blockers, and what is coming next.

Every engagement is assigned a dedicated project manager responsible for communication rhythm, stakeholder updates, and escalation paths. You will always have a named person to call.

Weekly Progress Reports
Written summary of sprint progress, completed items, blockers, and the plan for the coming week — delivered every Friday.
Live Project Dashboard
Real-time task board access (Jira, Asana, or your preferred tool) showing sprint velocity, open issues, and release schedule.
Fortnightly Stakeholder Calls
Structured 30-minute demo and review session with your project sponsor — not a status read-out, a working conversation about direction.
Shared Communication Channel
Direct Slack or Teams channel with the engineering team for ad-hoc questions — with agreed response time SLAs during working hours.
Milestone Sign-off Gates
Formal written sign-off at each phase boundary. Nothing advances to the next phase without explicit client approval.
Who Works on Your Project

The People Behind Every Delivery

Each project team is assembled specifically for the engagement — not pulled from a shared pool and context-switched between accounts.

Project Manager
Owns the timeline, communication, risk register, and client relationship. Your primary point of contact throughout the engagement.
Solution Architect
Designs the technical architecture, leads technology selection, and reviews all critical design decisions before they are implemented.
Senior Engineers
Front-end, back-end, mobile, or data engineers — matched to the specific stack and domain of the engagement, not assigned by availability.
QA Engineer
Writes and executes the test strategy, manages the defect register, and owns acceptance criteria tracking throughout the sprint cycle.

Engagement Models

Three Ways to Work With Us

We structure engagements to match how our clients actually operate — not to fit a billing template.

Fixed Scope
Project-Based Delivery
A defined SOW, fixed timeline, and fixed price. Ideal when requirements are clear and the outcome is well-specified. Change requests are managed through a formal process with costed amendments.
Best for: one-off builds, MVPs, defined integration projects.
See pricing details
Most Flexible
Dedicated Team
A full engineering pod — PM, architect, engineers, QA — assigned exclusively to your product. You direct the work; we provide the capability and process discipline. Scales up or down monthly.
Best for: ongoing product development, scaling an internal team, long-term partnerships.
Explore dedicated teams
Staff Augmentation
Engineers On Demand
Individual senior engineers placed directly into your team — working within your processes, using your tools, reporting to your leads. You get the talent without the recruitment overhead.
Best for: skill gaps, capacity surges, specialist roles difficult to hire locally.
Learn about augmentation
