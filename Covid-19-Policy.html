Covid-19 Safety Notice















 



 

 

Coronavirus(Covid-19)


Coronavirus remains a serious health risk. You should stay cautious to help protect yourself and others.


Training Information

As part of our current onboarding process, we provides basic level safety information which is detailed below. For all persons that are currently verified, please read the below information in order to stay safe:

  • Maintain Social Distance - Avoid large groups of people and keep a distance of at least 1m. Avoid non-essential travel

  • Self Isolate - Stay at home if you feel unwell. Avoid the risk of passing the risk of the virus to other people.

  • Indulge in regular cleaning practises -Regularly clean and disinfect surfaces, espiecially commonly used handles and switches.

  • Use your flex elbow - Cover your face and nose when you cough or sneeze to protect others.

  • Washing Hands and Personal Hygiene - Wash your hands reqularly espiecially after using the toilets. Avoid touching your face at all times. Remain clean at all times, shower regularly and use suitable deodorants.

Reporting Symptoms If you begin to feel unwell, or in fact you are having to self isolate due to coming in to contact with someone showing symptoms, then please follow the below advice:

  • Stay at home and self isolate for a period of 10 days if you are showing symptoms. If you live with someone with symptoms or have come into contact with someone who is showing symptoms then you will need to self isolate for 10 days.

  • Notify us immediately of your situation by contacting us on the Live Chat function within the website. It is your responsibility to inform us of your situation in order to minimise the risk of transmission

 

Let us know exactly what the situation is so that we can determine the period of quarantine

  • The GreaterHeight team will then suspend your profile for the period of quarantine in order to allow you to follow government advice.


  • Fully vaccinated people will no longer have to self isolate if they are identified as a close contact of someone with Covid (as long as they have no symptoms) Instead, you will need to take a PCR test and update us with the results. You can stop self-isolating if the result is negative. If the result is positive, you will need to continue self-isolating.

    Please note you will need to provide evidence of your vaccination.

  • The GreaterHeight team will then have a duty to ensure that all clients and fellow colleagues that you would have come into contact with immediately prior, are traced and contacted using our technology.


You must self-isolate if you or someone you live with have Covid symptoms. You can stop self-isolating if the person with symptoms gets a negative PCR result.
