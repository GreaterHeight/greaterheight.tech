Jobs and Career at GreaterHeight Technologies

GreaterHeight Technologies. A leading in Web solutions and outsourcing of Web design & promotion services for small to large scale businesses in Lagos, Nigeria. Offer services by combining web designing, marketing, and technology skills to assist clients in successfully using the internet for their marketing initiatives.

GreaterHeight Technologies strives diligently to foster an entrepreneurial work environment in which employees’ ideas are instrumental in the ongoing development of the company and its processes. It is in this manner that our employees truly have a stake in our success.

As a fast-growing company, GreaterHeight Technologies is looking for talented, career-oriented individuals to join our team. If you have the ability to observe the world from a creative point of view and you have a dynamic and ‘up’ personality, you may be the person we are looking for.
