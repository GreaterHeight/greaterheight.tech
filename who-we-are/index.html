Our Story

A Company Built on a Long View

GreaterHeight Technologies was incorporated in Lagos, Nigeria in June 2010 with a conviction that African enterprises deserved software solutions as sophisticated as any in the world — built locally, with global standards.

What began as a small consultancy has grown into a full-spectrum technology company: designing products, engineering platforms, analysing data, deploying AI and marketing the outcomes for clients across Africa, North America, Europe and the Gulf.

Our name captures the direction we are always moving — for our clients, our team and the communities we are part of.

Full Company Overview

Incorporated as an IT consulting and software development firm, serving Nigeria's growing technology market.
2014
Product Development Wing
Launched internal product development, resulting in DigitalSIWES, SearchArtisans and eCertMan.
2018
International Expansion
Opened offices in the USA and UK, bringing delivery capability closer to diaspora and global clients.
2023–Now
AI & Data Practice
Scaled an AI consulting and data engineering practice to help clients leverage generative AI and modern data infrastructure.
Core Purpose

Why We Exist

Technology is not our product — growth is. Every engagement at GreaterHeight is oriented around one question: how does this solution move our client closer to the height they were made to reach?

That conviction shapes every scoping session, every architecture decision and every delivery milestone.

Our Values

The Standards We Hold Ourselves To

These are not aspirations — they are the working principles that govern how we engage every day.

01

Integrity Without Exception

We say what we mean, deliver what we promise and tell clients what they need to hear — not just what they want to hear.

02

Craft Over Commodity

We build things to last. Every line of code, every design decision and every data model reflects a commitment to quality that outlives the project.

03

Client Growth First

Our success is measured by the measurable progress our clients make — not by billable hours or deliverable counts.

04

Continuous Learning

Technology moves fast. We maintain a disciplined culture of learning so our team always brings current, relevant thinking to every engagement.

05

Inclusive Excellence

Great ideas come from everywhere. We build diverse teams and create environments where every contributor's judgement is respected.

06

Long-Term Thinking

We are not interested in quick wins that create long-term debt. Every recommendation we make considers the three-year consequence, not just the immediate outcome.

Why GreaterHeight

What Makes the Difference

There are many technology firms. Here is what distinguishes how we work.

African Context, Global Standards

We understand the infrastructure realities, regulatory landscape and market dynamics of African business — and we build for them without compromising on international quality benchmarks.

Full-Spectrum Capability

From strategy through engineering to go-to-market, we cover the full technology value chain. Clients do not need to manage four agencies to get one outcome.

Delivery Certainty

Our structured delivery methodology, fixed-milestone models and transparent reporting mean clients always know where their project stands — and why.

Products We Have Built Ourselves

We are not just consultants — we are product builders. DigitalSIWES, SearchArtisans and GreaterHeight Academy mean we bring the discipline of product ownership into every client engagement.

Relationship Over Transaction

Most of our business comes from existing clients extending engagements and referring peers. That retention rate is the most honest signal of the quality we deliver.

Fifteen Years Without Pivoting

Since 2010 we have stayed focused on technology for growth. That consistency gives clients a partner with deep, accumulated expertise — not a company reinventing itself each year.
