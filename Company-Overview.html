ABOUT GREATERHEIGHT

IT Consulting & Outsourcing Services

GreaterHeight is a global IT consulting and outsourcing service provider in Nigeria, creating leading-edge solutions and services for individuals, organizations, and businesses throughout the world to help them succeed by turning them into a successful and high-performing brand, through the use of its resources, services, and solutions. We provide bespoke enterprise solutions that cater to various technology platforms across diverse industry domains worldwide from agriculture, banking & fintech, healthcare, logistics, real estate, travel and hospitality, retail and eCommerce, media, and entertainment, education and eLearning, automotive, and government.

Professional Industry Experts

GreaterHeight as a professional company of industry experts with exceptional experience leverages a comprehensive intelligence system across diverse industries and domains, tools, platforms, technologies, combined skills, knowledge, and experiences of its knowledge workers and industry partnerships to deliver the next generation, highly innovative business, and IT services.

Our Metamorphosis

GreaterHeight Technologies metamorphosed from GreaterHeights Consulting founded in 1999. GreaterHeight Technologies is headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria with consultants and a few liaising agencies around the world. The intention is to enable clients globally take advantage of the company’s services with the least possible stress.

Client Base

Because we live and breathe our core values, mission, and purpose, we have recorded 95% retention of our 90 clients in Africa, US, UK, and Europe with the help of our approximately 75+ employees, partners, and associates.

Global Areas of Competences

To date, GreaterHeight has developed or contributed to more than 40 software products in the market for small, medium, and large clients. Our stakeholders rely on us for innovative solutions, expertise, and services in our areas of competences: - e-commerce, mobile applications, cloud computing, digital transformation, business and data analytics, enterprise application integration and enterprise resource planning, Internet of Things (IoT), blockchain, big data, machine learning, artificial intelligence.
