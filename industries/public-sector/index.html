Public Sector Technology Challenges

Fragmented Citizen Records

Multiple agencies holding duplicate, inconsistent citizen data — preventing effective service delivery and benefit administration.

Paper-Based Processes

Manual workflows for permits, licences, and registrations that create bottlenecks, corruption risks, and poor citizen experience.

Procurement Complexity

FMFBNP procurement regulations and BPP requirements that demand transparent, auditable technology spending.

Data Security

Sensitive citizen data requiring strict access controls, audit logs, and compliance with NDPR and NITDA data governance frameworks.

Interoperability

Systems that cannot share data across ministries, departments, and agencies — duplicating effort and creating service gaps.

Citizen Trust

History of failed government technology projects that have eroded public confidence in digital government services.

Our Public Sector Capabilities

Citizen Portal Development

Self-service web and mobile portals for permits, licences, registrations, tax payments, and benefits — accessible on low-end devices.

E-Government Platforms

End-to-end digitisation of government services — from application intake through workflow management, decision support, and certificate issuance.

Identity & Registry Systems

Secure citizen identity management, biometric integration, and enterprise registries with de-duplication and data quality controls.

Interoperability Middleware

API gateways and integration layers that enable data sharing across ministries and agencies while respecting data sovereignty.

Revenue Management Systems

Electronic billing, payment processing, and reconciliation for IGR — integrated with Remita and state treasury systems.

Data Governance & Analytics

NDPR-compliant data warehouses and dashboards that give policy makers real-time visibility of service delivery metrics.
