Solomon Kugbe

Head, Corporate Governance


Mr. Solomon Kugbe is a multi-skilled Finance and Investment professional and an astute people-manager. He served, prior to his involvement with GreaterHeight, as the Chief Stockbroker at Almond Consult Ltd. Solomon, a duly registered stock broker by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and The Nigerian Stock Exchange (NSE), was responsible for over N12 Billion deals performed directly and through proxy by the brooking firm.

Every great company needs a numbers man. We're lucky to have one of the better ones in the country. Solomon has a hawk-eye, he audits our numbers and ensures we keep the structure of the business tight, together and as solid as can be.

He has over 25 years of extensive and rich experience that cut across Banking and Finance, Change Management and Stock Brooking, corporate governance, business partnering, mergers and acquisitions, treasury and funding, investor relations, cost management, financial operations, taxation, financial accounting, and reporting.

Having the genes of Finance and Investment, he very crafty with know “how” for shareholder value creation, business advisory, start-up and consulting

He has business interests in various segments of the economy such as agribusiness, ICT, real estates, and social entrepreneurship. He is also a professional Network Marketer working with reputable global companies and great brands.

At GreaterHeight, his marketing savvy, internal administration and industry research, are leveraged to provide oversight for leading GreaterHeight burgeoning strategy and financial discipline and hugely contribute to sharpening the overall strategic vision and approach of the company.

He superbly manages and executes the implementation of our company policy, methodologies, governance and best practices; while prioritizing the best interest of the workforce - whom he considers as the most valuable asset of the Company.

Solomon has a Master of Business Administration (MBA) degree from the prestigious University of Liverpool, specializing in Marketing; B.Sc. in Banking and Finance from Ogun State University

When Solomon isn’t working he enjoys pulling insights from sacred and wisdom books for life strategies with his wife children.
