GDPR & Data Protection

Please read this document carefully. It applies to all users of GreaterHeight Technologies websites and services.

Introduction & Scope

This GDPR & Data Protection notice explains how GreaterHeight Technologies Limited handles personal data in compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) 2016/679 and the UK GDPR as retained in UK law by the Data Protection Act 2018.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, the GDPR and UK GDPR respectively give you specific rights regarding your personal data. This notice describes those rights and how you can exercise them. It supplements our Privacy Policy, which sets out in full how we collect and use personal data across our website and services.

This notice applies to personal data processed by GreaterHeight Technologies Limited in connection with our website at https://greaterheight.tech, our services, and our business communications. It does not apply to the personal data of our own employees, for which separate internal policies apply.

Who We Are: The Data Controller

For the purposes of the GDPR and UK GDPR, the data controller responsible for your personal data is:

GreaterHeight Technologies Limited
2 Hakeem Dickson Street, Lekki Phase 1
Lagos, Nigeria

Email: privacy@greaterheight.tech

As a controller, we determine the purposes and means of processing your personal data. Where we engage third-party service providers to process personal data on our behalf, those parties act as processors and are bound by appropriate data processing agreements.

Legal Bases for Processing

We process personal data only where we have a lawful basis for doing so under Article 6 of the GDPR. The legal bases we rely on are:

Performance of a contract — where processing is necessary to provide a service you have requested or to take steps at your request before entering into a contract. For example, when you contact us about a project or request a proposal, we process your details to respond to and fulfil that request.

Legitimate interests — where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights. This includes website analytics (to understand how visitors use our site), security monitoring (to protect against fraud and abuse), and direct marketing to existing clients about related services.

Consent — where you have given us clear consent to process your data for a specific purpose, such as subscribing to our newsletter or agreeing to the placement of non-essential cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Compliance with a legal obligation — where processing is necessary for us to comply with a legal obligation under Nigerian, UK, or EU law, such as record-keeping obligations or responding to a lawful request from a public authority.

Your Rights Under the GDPR

Subject to applicable law, individuals in the EEA and UK have the following rights regarding their personal data:

Right of access — you have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how we process it. This is commonly known as a "subject access request."

Right to rectification — you have the right to ask us to correct personal data we hold about you that is inaccurate or incomplete.

Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — you may request that we delete your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, where you withdraw consent and there is no other legal basis for processing, or where you have successfully objected to processing.

Right to restriction of processing — you have the right to ask us to restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances — for example, while we verify a dispute about the accuracy of your data.

Right to data portability — where processing is based on your consent or on a contract, and is carried out by automated means, you have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and to have it transmitted to another controller where technically feasible.

Right to object — you have the right to object to the processing of your personal data where we rely on legitimate interests as our legal basis. We will cease processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing that override your interests, rights, and freedoms, or for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims. You may object to direct marketing at any time without providing any reason.

Rights related to automated decision-making — you have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing — including profiling — that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you. We do not currently carry out such processing, but we will inform you if this changes.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of the rights described above, please submit a written request to privacy@greaterheight.tech. We will acknowledge your request promptly and respond within one calendar month of receiving it. In complex cases or where we receive a high volume of requests, we may extend this period by a further two months, in which case we will notify you of the extension and the reason for it within the first month.

There is no charge for making a request. However, we may charge a reasonable administrative fee or refuse to act on a request that is manifestly unfounded or excessive, particularly where it is repetitive in nature. If we refuse your request, we will explain our reasons and inform you of your right to complain to the relevant supervisory authority and to seek judicial remedy.

We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. We will ask for the minimum information necessary to do so and will not use it for any other purpose.

International Data Transfers

GreaterHeight Technologies Limited is headquartered in Nigeria and operates globally. When we transfer personal data outside the EEA or UK to countries not recognised as providing an adequate level of data protection, we put in place appropriate safeguards to ensure your data remains protected.

Such safeguards may include the use of standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission or the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), binding corporate rules, or reliance on an approved adequacy decision where one exists. We carry out transfer impact assessments where required to ensure those mechanisms provide effective protection in practice.

You may request a copy of the safeguards we have put in place for any specific transfer by contacting privacy@greaterheight.tech.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purpose of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. To determine the appropriate retention period, we consider the amount, nature, and sensitivity of the data, the potential risk of harm from unauthorised use or disclosure, the purposes for which we process the data, and the applicable legal requirements.

In general, contact and enquiry data is retained for up to three years from last contact; client relationship data is retained for the duration of the relationship and for up to seven years thereafter in line with statutory accounting obligations; and website analytics data is retained in aggregated or anonymised form indefinitely, with underlying personal identifiers removed after 26 months. We review our retention schedules regularly and update them as our processing activities change.

Where you have asked us to delete your data, we will retain only the minimum information necessary to record that a deletion request was made and fulfilled, in order to demonstrate compliance with our legal obligations.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your country of residence or place of work if you believe we have not complied with our obligations under the GDPR or UK GDPR. We would, however, appreciate the opportunity to address your concerns before you contact the supervisory authority, and we encourage you to reach out to us first at privacy@greaterheight.tech.

If you are located in the United Kingdom, the relevant supervisory authority is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), which you can contact at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the relevant supervisory authority is the data protection authority in your EU member state of habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement. A full list of EU data protection authorities is available at edpb.europa.eu.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this GDPR & Data Protection notice from time to time to reflect changes in law, regulation, or our data processing activities. Any material changes will be indicated by an updated "Last updated" date at the bottom of this page. Where required by law, we will notify you of significant changes by other means, such as by email or by placing a prominent notice on our website.

We encourage you to review this notice periodically. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acknowledgement that you have read and understood the updated notice.

If you have any questions about this notice, about how we handle your personal data, or if you wish to exercise any of your rights, please contact us at: privacy@greaterheight.tech.
