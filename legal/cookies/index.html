Cookie Policy

We use cookies to make this website work well and to understand how people use it. This page explains what cookies we set, why, and how to control them.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They serve a range of purposes — from keeping you logged in to helping us understand which pages are most popular. Most websites use cookies; our use of them is described below.

Cookies are not executable files and cannot carry viruses or access information on your device beyond what you have provided to our website.

Cookies We Use

We use a small number of cookies, described in the table below. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not share cookie data with advertising networks.

Name Type Storage Duration Purpose
gh_cookies Necessary localStorage Until cleared Stores your cookie consent preference (accepted or necessary) so we do not show the consent banner on every visit.
gh_visited Necessary sessionStorage Session Records that you have visited the site in the current browser session to suppress a first-visit loading animation on subsequent page views within the same session.
Analytics cookies Analytics Cookie Up to 2 years Set only when you accept all cookies. Aggregate, anonymised usage data — pages visited and session duration — helps us improve the site. No personal data is collected.

Both gh_cookies and gh_visited use the browser's Web Storage API (localStorage and sessionStorage) rather than traditional HTTP cookies. They serve the same functional purpose and are subject to the same consent rules.

Your Cookie Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time using the buttons below. If you choose Only Necessary Cookies, we will not set analytics cookies and we will remove any that were previously set.

How Long We Keep Cookies

Session cookies (gh_visited) expire automatically when you close your browser. Persistent storage items (gh_cookies) remain until you clear your browser's localStorage or change your preferences on this page. Analytics cookies, where set, last up to two years.

Third-Party Cookies

We load fonts from Google Fonts when you visit this site. Google may set cookies or collect data in connection with this service. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details. We do not use any other third-party services that set cookies on your device.

Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can block or delete cookies using your browser settings. The links below provide guidance for the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Please note that blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of this and other websites you visit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when we change the cookies we use or when legislation requires it. We will post the updated policy on this page with the date of the most recent revision.
