ECERTMAN

 

Ecertman is a web-based solution that enables WAEC candidates – past, current and future – to apply for the printing of their WAEC certificates online. The solution enables the entire application process to be started and followed up online, from application to certificate drop-off at the candidate’s preferred location. This solution caters to a long-standing request for ease and mobility when processing WAEC certificates, for both WAEC and the candidate.
