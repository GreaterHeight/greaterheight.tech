OUR COMMUNITY SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITIES

About Greater Community

Greater Community is our sincere attempt to improve the health and quality of life of people. We are constantly nurturing our Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) program- Greater Community to identify, contribute, and take steps towards humanity focused on making a valuable difference.

With the Greater Community, our vision is to empower people & marginalized societies to thrive in a technology-driven economy and create exponential social and business impact. Our team aspires to connect socially & economically backward groups to resources with the ultimate goal of changing lives.

Corporate Social Responsibility Focus Areas

Feeding the Poor and there by eradicating hunger and malnutrition from communities

Clothing the needy through helping socially and economically backward groups from communities

Provision and Promoting Healthcare Initiatives within the needed communities

Providing Philanthropic Activities

Provision of care and Welfare for differently Disabled Persons in the society

Provision of Early Childhood Care and Education

Delibrate provision of Training and Mentoring the youth to become responsible

Our Approach

1. Engaging the Communities

2. Ensure Inclusive Growth

3. Build Trusting Relationships

4. Empower People

5. Ensure Sustainabilities
