A Range Of Business-Friendly Hiring Models Available


We offer our clients the sheer flexibility to choose the most appropriate hiring model suiting their business requirements.

 

 


Dedicated Team


If you represent a company with a project that needs dedicated attention, ask about dedicated teams. It’s a pay-as-you-go monthly rolling contract.


  • No hidden costs.

  • 160hrs of guaranteed production every month for part & full time.

  • Monthly billing.

  • Pay only for measurable work.

  • No setup fees.

  • Dedicated team saves time.

 


Fixed Price Model


If you represent a company with large well planned projects, ask about a fixed price model. It’s one time fixed price payment contract.


  • No hidden costs.

  • 160hrs of guaranteed production every month for part & full time.

  • One time billing.

  • Project based payment.

  • No setup fees.

  • Free from project management.

 


Hourly

If you represent a company with undefined projects and need ongoing work, ask about hourly. It’s a pay-as-you-go hour wise rolling contract.


  • No hidden costs.

  • Work hours are decided as per project requirement.

  • Monthly billing.

  • Pay only for measurable work.

  • No setup fees.

  • Flexibility of working as per requirement.
