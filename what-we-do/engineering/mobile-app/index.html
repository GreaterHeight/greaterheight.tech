Product Engineering — Mobile

Apps that earn their place on the home screen

The standard for mobile is brutal: users delete apps that are slow, unreliable, or confusing. We build native iOS and Android applications — and cross-platform apps where the brief calls for it — that survive contact with real users on real networks.

iOS

Built for Apple — not ported to it

iOS users expect apps that feel native: fluid transitions, gesture-driven navigation, Haptic feedback precisely timed, and behaviour that matches the platform they know. We write iOS apps in Swift with SwiftUI, following Apple's Human Interface Guidelines as a starting point and departing from them only with intention.

Swift & SwiftUI

Modern, type-safe codebase with declarative UI that reduces complexity and simplifies future updates.

Native platform integrations

Face ID, Touch ID, HealthKit, CoreLocation, NFC, ARKit, StoreKit, Push Notifications, and Siri Shortcuts — used where the app genuinely benefits from them.

App Store submission & review

We manage the full submission process: signing, screenshots, metadata, age ratings, and responding to App Store review feedback.

iPhone, iPad, and Mac Catalyst

Apps designed for a primary form factor and extended thoughtfully to others, not stretched across screen sizes without adaptation.

Android

Native Android, built for the market

Android is the dominant platform in Nigeria and across the African continent. We build Android apps in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, targeting the actual device landscape: mid-range handsets, variable network quality, and users who expect performance on hardware that is not flagship.

Kotlin & Jetpack Compose

Google's modern Android stack: concise, coroutine-native, and significantly more maintainable than legacy Java codebases.

Device & OS fragmentation handling

We test across a range of real devices and Android versions, not just emulators. Performance targets are set for mid-range hardware.

Google Play publishing

Store listing optimisation, internal/closed/open testing track management, and production rollout with staged release.

USSD & SMS fallback patterns

For apps where network access is inconsistent, we design offline-first flows and USSD complement paths where appropriate.

What We Build

Mobile applications across every category

We work across the full range of mobile app types — consumer-facing products, enterprise tools, and everything in between.

Consumer Apps

Apps built for wide public adoption: clean onboarding, low friction, and engagement loops designed with the real context of daily use. We care deeply about first-launch experience and retention mechanics.

Enterprise & Internal Tools

Field apps, approval tools, inventory systems, and internal dashboards used by employees — not the public. These must be reliable, fast on enterprise networks, and simple enough for occasional users who have not been trained.

Fintech & Payments

Mobile wallets, loan apps, savings products, payment collection tools, and financial account management apps. We understand PCI-DSS considerations, biometric authentication, and the specific UX demands of financial transactions on a small screen.

Health & Wellness Apps

Patient-facing applications, appointment booking, health tracking, telemedicine companions, and healthcare workforce tools. We handle the specific data sensitivity requirements these applications carry.

Field & Logistics Apps

Delivery tracking, driver apps, inspection tools, survey collection, and logistics coordination. These must work in poor connectivity. We architect offline-first with sync, not online-first with error handling.

eCommerce & Marketplaces

Shopping apps, B2B ordering tools, marketplace platforms, and subscription commerce. Payment integration, cart state persistence, push notification re-engagement, and storefront performance are all primary concerns.

Our Standards

Performance, security, and store compliance — built in, not checked at the end

Mobile apps fail in ways that are more visible than web applications. A crash on a user's device is personal. A one-star review is public. We build to avoid both.

Performance profiling before submission

Memory usage, battery impact, cold start time, and frame rate measured on real hardware before any build goes to the stores.

Secure local storage

No sensitive data stored in plain text. We use the iOS Keychain, Android Keystore, and encrypted SharedPreferences where appropriate — not UserDefaults or generic local storage.

Certificate pinning on sensitive endpoints

For fintech, healthcare, and enterprise apps where the API handles sensitive data, certificate pinning prevents man-in-the-middle attacks on mobile networks.

Crash monitoring from day one

Crashlytics or Sentry configured before launch. We read crash reports, not user complaints, as our signal for what to fix first.

Common Questions

What to know before commissioning a mobile app

Native iOS (Swift/SwiftUI) or Android (Kotlin/Jetpack Compose) is the right choice when your app requires deep platform integration — camera, Bluetooth, NFC, offline sync, biometrics, background processing, or platform-specific UI conventions that users expect. Cross-platform (React Native or Flutter) works very well for apps where the core UI and logic are largely the same on both platforms, your team needs to ship quickly, and the app does not depend on cutting-edge platform APIs. We assess the specific requirements before recommending either direction.

Offline capability is an architecture decision made at the design stage, not a feature added afterward. We determine which data must be available offline, how conflicts between local and server state are resolved, and how synchronisation is triggered. Depending on the complexity, we use local SQLite databases, Core Data on iOS, Room on Android, or Redux Persist for React Native apps. For enterprise field apps where connectivity is unreliable, offline-first architecture is built as the foundation.
