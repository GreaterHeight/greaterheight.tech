Delivery Approach

Most software projects fail before a line of code is written.

Business goals, technical decisions, and project planning are rarely aligned from the beginning. That misalignment — not the difficulty of development — is the root cause of most delivery failures.

68%
of software projects miss budget or schedule targets
70%
of digital transformation projects fail to meet business goals
52%
of projects experience uncontrolled scope changes during delivery

Source: Standish Group CHAOS Report  ·  McKinsey Digital  ·  Deloitte Insights

Principle one

Business goals aligned before development starts

We document a clear understanding of business requirements, user needs, project scope, and technical priorities before a single line of production code is written. What we build is always traceable to what you need to achieve.

Principle two

Progress proven through working software, not completed tasks

Working features, business value, and user adoption matter more than development milestones. From sprint two, you see and test running software at the end of every two-week cycle — not a progress report on tasks checked off.

Principle three

No uncontrolled changes in requirements or delivery

Every requirement is signed off before build begins. Changes go through formal change control — assessed for impact, documented in writing, and approved before implementation. No surprise scope creep, no cost shocks late in the project.

Why Custom Software

Off-the-shelf solves the average problem.
Custom software solves yours.

Most businesses eventually hit the ceiling of what packaged software can do. When that happens, the choice is between adapting your business to the software — or building software that adapts to your business.

Off-the-shelf software
Built for the median use case — your specific workflows are an afterthought
Your team adapts to the software's logic, not the other way around
You pay for features you will never use — and miss the ones you need
The vendor controls your roadmap, your pricing, and your data model
Integration is an afterthought — done through workarounds and brittle connectors
When the vendor sunsets it, you start over — on their timeline
Custom software by GreaterHeight
Designed around your exact workflows, data model, and operational constraints
The software adapts to how your business operates — not the reverse
Only the features you need — no unused modules, no bloat, no licensing ceiling
You own the roadmap, you own the code, you own the competitive advantage
Integration is designed in from the start — not bolted on as an afterthought
You own the asset — it runs as long as your business needs it, under your control

Our Process

From requirement to running software

Custom software development fails most often when the build starts before the problem is fully understood. Our process is front-loaded: we invest heavily in requirements, architecture, and design before writing production code.

Phase 1

Discovery & Requirements

Deep-dive into your business processes, user needs, integration landscape, and constraints. We produce a detailed requirements specification before architecture begins.

Phase 2

Architecture & Design

Technology selection, system design, data modelling, API contracts, and UI/UX design. Architecture decisions are documented and client-approved before build begins.

Phase 3

Iterative Build

Agile sprints with working software demonstrated at the end of every cycle. You see and test real functionality from week two — not week twenty.

Phase 4

Testing & QA

Automated test suites, manual QA, performance testing, and security review. Every release candidate is tested against the original requirements.

Phase 5

Deployment & Handover

Production deployment, documentation, team training, and a defined support arrangement. You own your software completely — source code, infrastructure, and documentation.

Security & Ownership

Your software, your code, your data.
Protected from day one.

Security is not an afterthought added at the end of the project. IP protection, data governance, and access controls are designed into every engagement from the first working session.

100% IP Ownership — Contractual

Everything built for you belongs to you from the first commit. An NDA is signed before work begins. All ownership terms are clearly defined in every engagement contract — no retained rights, no licensing back to us, no access after the engagement ends.

Secure Development Practices

Repositories, cloud environments, credentials, and deployment pipelines are managed through controlled permissions and explicit access ownership. Every team member is onboarded with least-privilege access and offboarded completely when their role ends.

Data Protection & NDPR Compliance

Software handling personal data is built with NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation) requirements applied throughout. We advise on consent flows, data retention policies, and breach notification obligations — and provide data processing agreements where required.

Protection Beyond Documentation

Source code is delivered to your repository, not stored in ours after engagement. You retain the complete commit history of every change made to your system. Post-engagement, our access is fully revoked — you control who can read and modify your system.

Standards awareness: NDPR NITDA Guidelines CBN IT Standards ISO 27001 Framework OWASP Top 10 PCI-DSS (payments) GDPR (EU clients) HIPAA-aware (health)
