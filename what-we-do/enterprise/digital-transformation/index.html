Digital Transformation

Transformation is not a technology project. It is a business decision.

Digital transformation fails when it is treated as an IT initiative. We work at the intersection of business strategy and technology execution, ensuring that every system change produces a measurable business outcome.

Capability Areas

Four domains where transformation creates lasting value

Genuine transformation touches each of these domains. Some programmes begin in one; most of the most valuable ones eventually span all four. We help organisations understand where to start and how to sequence the work.

01

Customer Experience

Redesigning the full customer journey, from first contact through to retention. This covers digital channels, self-service capability, and the data infrastructure that makes personalisation possible.

  • Reduced service wait times
  • Higher digital adoption rates
  • Improved NPS scores
02

Operations & Process

Automating manual processes, integrating disconnected systems, and building the operational workflows that allow the business to do more without proportionally scaling headcount.

  • Lower cost per transaction
  • Fewer manual handoffs
  • Shorter processing cycles
03

Data & Intelligence

Building the data infrastructure and analytics capability that makes real-time decisions possible. This includes data collection, warehousing, reporting, and the AI tools that surface insights from that data.

  • Single source of truth
  • Predictive operational insight
  • Faster executive decisions
04

Culture & Capability

Ensuring the workforce can operate in a digital environment. Technology delivers nothing if the people using it do not change how they work. Training, change management, and new ways of working are part of every engagement.

  • Faster technology adoption
  • Reduced change resistance
  • Digital leadership capability
Our Philosophy

We separate what should be transformed from what should be optimised

Not everything in a business needs transformation. The word is overused and expensive. We work with leadership to separate the processes that need fundamental redesign from those that simply need improvement, so the investment goes where it creates the most value.

01

Strategy before technology

We define the business outcomes first. Technology is selected to serve those outcomes, not the reverse.

02

Governed delivery in phases

We work in time-boxed stages with clear success criteria, so the programme can be redirected if priorities change.

03

Change management included

People, process, and technology change together. We embed change management in every delivery phase, not as a parallel workstream.

04

African market grounding

We design for the operational realities of African enterprise: connectivity variability, regulatory diversity, and workforce dynamics that are distinct from Western programme assumptions.

Why GreaterHeight

We understand African enterprise from the inside

Domain-Led Delivery

Our engagements are led by people who understand your industry, not just technology. We bring industry knowledge to every diagnostic so we ask the questions your internal teams already know the importance of.

African Context by Design

We do not apply templates from other markets and hope they work here. Infrastructure realities, regulatory environments, and workforce dynamics in West Africa require different design decisions, and we account for them from day one.

Outcomes, Not Outputs

We measure ourselves against business outcomes: revenue generated, cost reduced, time saved, decisions improved. We build measurement into the engagement from the beginning so accountability is shared, not deferred.

Common Questions

What organisations ask before starting

A software development project delivers a defined system. A transformation engagement changes how the business operates. It includes strategy, change management, training, and ongoing governance — not just technology build. The technology is one component, not the whole thing.

Three failure patterns repeat: (1) starting with technology selection before the business problem is clear, (2) treating adoption as optional — assuming people will use new systems without support — and (3) measuring technology delivery rather than business outcomes. Our engagement model is designed around avoiding all three.

We work with your internal team, not around them. The goal is for your IT function to own the new capability at the end of the engagement. We bring specialist depth in areas your team may not have, while your team brings the institutional knowledge no outsider has. That combination is how transformation actually sticks.
