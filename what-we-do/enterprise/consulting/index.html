Enterprise & Advisory

The right advice at the right moment changes what is possible

Technology decisions made without adequate counsel are expensive to reverse. GreaterHeight Technologies provides advisory-led consulting for organisations navigating complex technology choices, capability gaps, or transitions in leadership.

Most organisations already know what their problem is. What they need is a trusted partner to confirm it, challenge it, and help them decide what to do about it without the bias of a vendor who benefits from the answer.

Engagement Models

Four ways to work with our advisory team

Each model is designed for a different stage of need. They can be combined, sequenced, or run in parallel depending on the complexity of your situation.

01 — Ongoing Advisory

Strategic Retainer Advisory

A defined number of advisory hours per month, available to your executive team. This includes participation in key technology decisions, review of vendor proposals, strategic direction on architecture and investment, and direct access to a senior GreaterHeight advisor. Relationships built over time produce better counsel than those established under pressure.

Ideal for: CEOs, CTOs, boards, and investors without full-time technology leadership
What is included
  • Monthly advisory sessions with a named senior consultant
  • On-call access for time-sensitive decisions
  • Vendor proposal review and negotiation guidance
  • Quarterly technology health summary
02 — Bounded Scope

Project-Based Consulting

A clearly scoped engagement with a defined deliverable, timeline, and fee. Appropriate when you have a specific question to answer or decision to validate: a build vs buy analysis, a vendor selection, a technology architecture review, or a roadmap for the next 12 months. The engagement ends when the deliverable is complete and accepted.

Ideal for: organisations facing a specific decision or capability gap with a defined timeframe
What is included
  • Scoping session to confirm objectives and success criteria
  • Research, analysis, and structured recommendations
  • Written report with executive summary
  • Presentation to leadership or board
03 — Interim Leadership

Interim CTO / CIO

Experienced technology leadership placed into your organisation for a defined period: while you recruit permanently, through a programme transition, during a critical phase where the existing leader is unavailable, or while a founder-CTO transitions to a different role. Our interim leaders operate as full members of your executive team, not as external consultants.

Ideal for: organisations in transition, post-fundraise scaling, or facing a leadership gap in technology
What is included
  • Full executive participation in leadership meetings
  • Team leadership and delivery oversight
  • Recruitment support and handover planning
  • Documentation of decisions and architecture for the incoming leader
04 — Independent Assessment

Technology Due Diligence

An independent assessment of a technology asset, software product, or vendor relationship on behalf of an investor, acquirer, or board. We examine the architecture, codebase quality, team capability, technical debt, security posture, and scalability of the asset, and provide a written report with risk ratings and recommendations. We have no commercial relationship with the subject of the assessment.

Ideal for: investors, acquirers, boards, and organisations considering significant vendor commitments
What is included
  • Architecture and codebase assessment
  • Technical debt and scalability analysis
  • Security and compliance posture review
  • Risk-rated report with remediation priorities

Advisory is most valuable when the relationship is built before the crisis. We work best with clients who bring us in early.

How We Work

Structured enough to be useful. Flexible enough to fit your reality.

Consulting engagements fail when they produce reports no one reads. Our process is designed around producing counsel that actually influences decisions, delivered through relationships built on candour rather than comfort.

01

Understand the actual question

The question an organisation thinks it has and the question it actually has are often different. We spend the first engagement clarifying what problem the advice needs to solve, and who has the authority to act on it.

02

Research without assumptions

We talk to the people who operate the systems, not just the people who commission the reports. Ground-level understanding changes the analysis. We draw on 20 years of Nigerian and African market experience, not imported frameworks.

03

Give an opinion, not a list of options

Consulting that presents five equally weighted options and leaves the decision entirely with the client is expensive neutrality. We form a view, explain the reasoning, and present it as a recommendation, while being transparent about the assumptions it rests on.

04

Stay available after the report

Decisions informed by our counsel often raise new questions. We remain accessible after delivery to help interpret, refine, or update our recommendations as reality changes, without treating each conversation as a new billable event.

Our Advisory Domains

Where we bring substantive knowledge, not just process

Useful consulting depends on having genuine expertise in the domain being advised on. These are the areas where GreaterHeight brings depth developed over real engagements.

Technology Architecture Review

Assessment of your current architecture against your stated business direction. We identify structural risks, unnecessary complexity, and the highest-priority improvements before they become failures.

Vendor Selection & Evaluation

Independent evaluation of technology vendors, software platforms, and system integrators. We develop requirements, design the evaluation framework, review proposals, and provide a recommendation with supporting rationale.

IT Governance & Risk

Assessment and design of IT governance structures: decision rights, policy frameworks, security controls, compliance requirements, and risk management processes appropriate for your organisation's size and sector.

Build vs Buy Analysis

A structured assessment of whether a capability should be built internally, purchased as a product, or sourced through integration. We model total cost of ownership, strategic flexibility, and time-to-value across each option.

Technology Investment Planning

Advice on how to allocate a technology budget across run, grow, and transform activities. We help leadership understand what they are spending and what it is producing, and design an investment portfolio with clearer return expectations.

Digital Strategy

Development of a coherent digital strategy aligned to the business strategy. This is not a list of technology initiatives but a framework for which capabilities to invest in, in which sequence, and by what measure the investment has worked.

The Case for Independent Counsel

Most technology decisions are made with insufficient challenge

Internal teams are close to the problem and subject to organisational pressures. Vendors have a commercial interest in the answer. An independent advisor has neither constraint. We can say what internal teams cannot say and assess what vendors will not disclose.

Who We Work With

The organisations that benefit most from independent technology counsel

Enterprises Without a CTO

Organisations that have grown to a scale where technology decisions are consequential but have not yet hired a full-time technology leader. A retainer advisory arrangement provides board-level counsel without the cost or commitment of a permanent appointment.

Organisations Facing a Major Technology Decision

Businesses about to make a significant technology investment, replace a core system, select an enterprise software platform, or sign a long-term vendor contract. The cost of independent counsel is small relative to the cost of the wrong decision.

Private Equity and Investors

Investment firms that need an independent technical assessment of a software-intensive business before close, or portfolio companies that need help governing their technology function without hiring a full CTO at each portfolio entity.

Founders and Scaling Businesses

Companies that have outgrown their founding technology architecture and need help planning the transition to systems that will carry them through the next phase of growth, without pausing operations or starting from scratch.
