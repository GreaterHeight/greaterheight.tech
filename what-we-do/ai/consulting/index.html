Most AI projects fail not for lack of ambition but for absence of structure. GreaterHeight helps you identify where AI genuinely creates value, build the data and infrastructure foundations it requires, and move from proof-of-concept to reliable production system.

Our Approach

We evaluate AI opportunities against your actual data maturity, operational context, and business model — not against what vendors are selling this quarter.

What We Deliver

A grounded implementation roadmap with prioritised use cases, architecture decisions, build-vs-buy recommendations, and clear success metrics.

What We Do

AI consulting grounded in engineering reality

AI consulting without engineering depth produces roadmaps that cannot be built. At GreaterHeight, our AI consulting practice is led by engineers who have shipped AI systems to production — people who understand the gap between a well-performing model and a reliable, maintainable product.

We begin every engagement with an honest assessment of where you are: your data quality and availability, your team's current capabilities, your infrastructure, and the specific business outcomes you need. From there we design an AI adoption path that is proportionate to your situation — not a framework lifted from a global consultancy and applied regardless of context.

We work across all major AI providers and frameworks, and our recommendations are independent. We tell you what to build, what to buy, and what to avoid — and why.

Consulting Services

What an engagement covers

AI Readiness Assessment

A structured evaluation of your data assets, infrastructure, team capabilities, and existing processes. Produces an honest gap analysis and prioritised action list before any build work begins.

Use Case Identification & Prioritisation

We map AI opportunities to your actual business model — identifying where automation, prediction, recommendation, or generation create measurable value and where they do not.

Architecture & Technology Selection

Vendor-neutral guidance on models, platforms, infrastructure, and tooling. We help you avoid costly lock-in and build an AI stack that your engineering team can own and maintain.

Implementation Roadmap

A phased plan with sequenced use cases, resource requirements, integration dependencies, risk considerations, and measurable milestones that connect AI investment to business outcomes.

Data Strategy

AI is only as good as the data underneath it. We assess your data pipelines, labelling, governance, and quality frameworks — and design the data infrastructure your AI initiatives require.

Build vs Buy Evaluation

Rigorous analysis of when to build custom models, when to fine-tune foundational models, and when to use off-the-shelf AI APIs — with total cost of ownership as a central consideration.
