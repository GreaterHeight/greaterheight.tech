GreaterHeight offers Enterprise Content Management services that provide solutions for your content-driven business processes across the enterprise. Our ECM solutions provide new value from previously unstructured and unavailable data.

When effectively managed across the organization, content can be used to engage customers, automate business processes, and enhance collaboration. To enable a successful Enterprise Content Management (ECM) solution implementation, we offer a suite of advisory and consulting services to help evaluate readiness and create a roadmap.

Our Enterprise content management (ECM) solutions enable an organization to leverage customer information and company knowledge in its content.

A Range Of Business-Friendly Hiring Models Available


We offer our clients the sheer flexibility to choose the most appropriate hiring model suiting their business requirements.

 

 


Dedicated Team


If you represent a company with a project that needs dedicated attention, ask about dedicated teams. It’s a pay-as-you-go monthly rolling contract.


  • No hidden costs.

  • 160hrs of guaranteed production every month for part & full time.

  • Monthly billing.

  • Pay only for measurable work.

  • No setup fees.

  • Dedicated team saves time.

 


Fixed Price Model


If you represent a company with large well planned projects, ask about a fixed price model. It’s one time fixed price payment contract.


  • No hidden costs.

  • 160hrs of guaranteed production every month for part & full time.

  • One time billing.

  • Project based payment.

  • No setup fees.

  • Free from project management.

 


Hourly

If you represent a company with undefined projects and need ongoing work, ask about hourly. It’s a pay-as-you-go hour wise rolling contract.

  • No setup fees.

  • Flexibility of working as per requirement.
